Workflow

From points to parcel
in three steps.

01

Import Your Survey Data

Load existing survey points from .pnt, .csv, or .txt files — or enter them manually into the built-in editor. All formats are supported out of the box.

02

Calculate & Visualize

Hit Calculate and instantly see the computed parcel area alongside a real-time interactive plot of your polygon. Adjust points and watch results update live.
