Engineered for surveyors.

Everything you need to map, calculate, and report.

📊

Command Center Dashboard

A unified interface giving you immediate access to your recent projects, quick-start templates, and live system metrics. No clutter, just the tools you need.

📐

Advanced Area Calculation

Compute parcel areas with algorithmic precision. Handles complex geometries, curved boundaries, and coordinate transformations seamlessly.

⚡

Integrated AI Assistant

Stuck on a workflow? The built-in context-aware AI assistant guides you through complex calculations and software configurations in real-time.

📍

Real-time Visual Plotting

See your survey points come to life. The interactive plotting engine instantly visualizes your data as you enter or edit points. Export high-fidelity diagrams directly to PDF for client reports.

📁

Robust Data Management

Auto-save functionality ensures you never lose a coordinate. Organize files efficiently with built-in versioning and rapid retrieval.

📝

Universal Points Editor

Import from or export to standard survey formats (.txt, .csv, .pnt) with absolute reliability and lightning-fast parsing.

⚙️

Global Configuration

Switch instantly between metric and imperial units, adjust precision limits, and define global coordinate systems.
